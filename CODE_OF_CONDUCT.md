# Code of Conduct

OpenPhoneUse wants its issues, pull requests and discussions to be a place where anyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback that helps the other person improve their work.
- Accept feedback on your own work gracefully.
- Write so that newcomers and people reading in a second language can follow.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, an email or a screenshot of their phone, without their permission.
- Using this project or its issue tracker to help someone harm another person, for example by asking for ways to make the agent act on a phone without its owner's consent.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, and anywhere someone speaks on behalf of the project.

## Reporting a problem

Email the maintainer at [joungminsung080830@gmail.com](mailto:joungminsung080830@gmail.com), or use the repository's [private report form](https://github.com/joungminsung/openphoneuse/security/advisories/new). Only the maintainer reads what you send. Say what happened and link to it if you can. For abuse that breaks GitHub's own rules, you can also use the "Report content" option on the comment itself.

The maintainer will read every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and reply with what was decided.

## What happens next

Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before, the maintainer may ask for a correction, edit or remove the content, give a warning, or block the account from the project, for a time or for good.
